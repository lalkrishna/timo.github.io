Timo

Built around one number: how many days.

Everything in Timo exists to get that number right, keep it in front of you, and nudge you before the day arrives.

Add an event with just a name

Type a name and save. That’s all Timo needs. Add a date, a time, a reminder or a photo now or later.

Type the date into the name, like “Goa trip 19 Dec”, and Timo sets it for you.

Counts down, then counts up

Every event counts down until its day arrives, then keeps counting up afterwards. On the day of a timed event, Timo counts down in hours, then minutes.

Reminders you can trust

  • On the day, 1, 3 or 7 days before, or on a day and time you choose.
  • See exactly when each reminder will arrive before you save it.
  • Reminders stay correct after a restart, a time zone change or a clock change.

Make each event yours

Add a photo from your gallery or camera, or pick one of 8 gradients. Until you choose, Timo gives each event a gradient based on its name.

Pin any event to your home screen with the widget, in three sizes. It updates on its own at midnight.

Your dates stay on your phone.

Timo has no account, no ads and no tracking. It doesn’t have permission to use the internet, so your events can’t leave your phone.

Read the privacy policy

  • No sign-up. Open the app and start counting.
  • Nothing collected. No analytics, no crash-reporting tools, no advertising ID.
  • Photos without location. Timo saves a resized copy of your photo with its location and camera details removed.
  • Not in cloud backup. This first version keeps everything on this phone only, so uninstalling the app removes your events.
